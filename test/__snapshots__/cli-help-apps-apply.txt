Show what siren.app.json changes, ask, then declare the app and save its key
beside the file.

USAGE
  siren apps apply [file] [flags]

FLAGS
  --yes               Skip the question. Needed with no terminal
  --json              Print the plan and the app as JSON

GLOBAL FLAGS
  --sandbox, --live, --base-url, --org, --quiet, --verbose and --help work with
  every command. siren --help explains them.

EXAMPLES
  $ siren apps apply
  $ siren apps apply --live --yes

LEARN MORE
  Read the guide at https://github.com/Novatorius/siren-node#command-line-tool
