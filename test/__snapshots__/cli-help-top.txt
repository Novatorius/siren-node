Declare Siren apps, and watch and answer what Siren sends them.

USAGE
  siren <command> [flags]

GETTING STARTED
  signup              Make an organization on Siren
  login               Log in to Siren in your browser
  apps init           Write siren.app.json in this folder
  apps apply          Declare the app on Siren and save its key

APPS
  apps list           List the apps
  apps show           Show one app and its features
  apps rotate-key     Replace the app's key and revoke the old one
  apps rotate-secret  Replace the address's signing secret
  apps add-key        Make a key for an app that has none
  apps on             Turn the app on
  apps off            Turn the app off
  apps delete         Delete the app and revoke its key

REQUESTS
  apps requests       List what Siren sent, or show one request
  apps answer         Answer an ask by hand
  apps resend         Send a request again
  listen              Send the app's requests to this machine

ACCOUNT
  logout              Log out, and forget the session here
  whoami              Show who you are logged in as
  orgs                List your organizations

FLAGS
  --sandbox           Use Siren's sandbox, whatever you logged in to last
  --live              Log in to or change a live organization
  --base-url <url>    Use another Siren, such as siren.example.com
  --org <slug>        The organization to act in, when you have several
  -q, --quiet         Skip the logo animation
  --verbose           Show the detail behind an error
  -h, --help          Show help for a command
  -v, --version       Show the version

ENVIRONMENT
  SIREN_BASE_URL      The Siren to use when no flag names one
  SIREN_ORG           The organization to act in, when no flag names one
  SIREN_TOKEN         A personal access token, for scripts and CI
  SIREN_DEBUG=1       The same as --verbose
  NO_COLOR, CI        Plain output, and no logo animation

EXAMPLES
  $ siren login
  $ siren apps init
  $ siren apps apply

LEARN MORE
  Run siren <command> --help for a command's flags and examples.
  Read the guide at https://github.com/Novatorius/siren-node#command-line-tool
