Log in by approving a code in your browser.

USAGE
  siren login [flags]

FLAGS
  --password          Log in with your email and password, with no browser
  --password-stdin    Read the password from standard input, for scripts
  --email <address>   The email for --password, asked when left out

GLOBAL FLAGS
  --sandbox, --live, --base-url, --org, --quiet, --verbose and --help work with
  every command. siren --help explains them.

EXAMPLES
  $ siren login
  $ siren login --password
